$ post-raise-plan --help

Before you start.

How much of my time does this take?

About a day over 3 weeks: a kickoff, a review of the draft plan, and the planning session with your team. Everything else is drafted for you.

Why not make a senior hire instead?

Plan first, then hire. A COO without a plan waits on you. The plan tells you if you need one, when, and what they’d own, so if you do hire, they know their job from day one.

Isn’t this what a fractional COO does?

A fixed scope, and no months of diagnosis first: a finished plan in 3 weeks, built with your own team, then 90 days of running it with you. I’ve spent 3+ years in the operator seat at a funded scale-up, making these calls with investors’ money on the line, and I’ve worked with 20+ founders on the moment you’re in now.

My investors already help. Why add someone?

Good, keep them close. This gives them a plan to back, built with the people who’ll run it, so every board update starts from progress against something they’ve already seen.

I have a deck and a model. Isn’t that the plan?

The deck is the promise. The plan turns it into work, order and owners, so your team moves without waiting on you and you can show your board exactly where you stand.

We’re staying lean. Isn’t this overhead?

It’s how you stay lean. Every hire and every dollar has to earn its place against a milestone, so you skip the expensive mistakes: the senior hire you didn’t need yet, and the spend that moves nothing.

Can’t I just do this with AI?

AI will write you a plan in 5 minutes. It won’t sit with your team, find where the deck and the people disagree, or make the hard calls with you, and those decide whether the plan gets run.

I already have a coach. Do I need this too?

Keep your coach. You have to grow as fast as the company does, and that’s their work. This is about the company: a plan your team runs, with an owner and a budget on every piece of work.

What if my team doesn’t buy in?

They build it with me, so it’s their plan and you’re not the one pushing it every week. If your leadership team doesn’t sign off on the plan at the week 3 planning session, I keep working at no extra cost until they do.

What does it cost?

A fixed fee, usually less than a recruiter’s fee for one senior hire. It depends on how many people are involved, and how much help you want running it in the 90 days. You’ll have the number before anything starts.

What happens after the 90 days?

Your team runs the plan and re-plans every quarter on the way to the next round. If you want me around for that, I stay on as an advisor.

When should I start?

Before the big hires and spend lock in, ideally in the first 90 days after your Series A.

We closed months ago. Am I too late?

No. Earlier is easier. If you’ve already hired ahead of a plan, the plan shows what each of those people should own now.

Who isn’t this for?

The Post-Raise Plan starts after a Series A. Haven’t raised yours yet? Come back after the round. And if you want advice without changing how you work, this won’t help.

I’m not in Europe. Does that work?

Yes. I’ve worked with clients from San Francisco to Hong Kong. Most of the work is async, and there’s always a time that works for a call.
